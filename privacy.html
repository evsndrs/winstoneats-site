Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026

Explore WS is an independent app for finding food, coffee, drinks, food trucks and events in Winston-Salem, North Carolina. It's run by Evan Saunders ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with information about you. We've kept it short because we collect very little.

The short version

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • Your location is used on your phone to show what's near you. We never receive or store it.
  • Your favorites are saved only on your phone.
  • If you send a "Report wrong info" message, we receive what you write (and your email only if you choose to add it).
  • We don't sell or share your information with advertisers or data brokers.

Location

If you allow it, the app uses your device's location while you're using it to sort and filter places by distance (for example, "Near me"). That happens on your device. Your location isn't sent to us, stored by us, or shared with anyone. You can turn location access off anytime in iPhone Settings → Explore WS → Location; the app still works without it.

Favorites and settings

Spots you star and your app settings are stored only on your device. We can't see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

"Report wrong info"

If you tap "Report wrong info" on a place or food truck and send a report, we receive:

  • which place or truck it's about and the issue you picked,
  • anything you type in the message box,
  • your email address, only if you choose to enter one so we can reply,
  • the app's version number and the time of the report.

We use reports only to correct listings and, if you gave an email, to reply to you. Reports aren't tied to your device or any account. We keep them as long as needed to fix the listing. To have a report you sent deleted, email us (below).

Services the app uses

To show listings, the app connects to these services. Like any website, they receive standard technical details with each request (such as your IP address and device type), and they handle those under their own privacy policies.

  • Supabase hosts the Explore WS database of places, food trucks, events and weekly happenings, and stores reports you send.
  • Google: when you open a place, our server asks Google for that place's current rating and reviews. The request contains the place's name and location, not information about you. Ratings and reviews shown are from Google.
  • Apple Maps draws maps and looks up addresses of events and food trucks so they can be placed on the map.
  • Event images are loaded from the organizer's website (for example, a museum or university calendar).

When you tap Directions, a website, Instagram, a phone number or tickets, you leave Explore WS and that app or site's privacy policy applies.

Children

Explore WS isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change how the app handles information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
