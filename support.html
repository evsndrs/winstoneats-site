Support

Explore WS: food, coffee, drinks, food trucks and events in Winston-Salem, NC.

Something wrong in the app? Open the place or food truck and tap Report wrong info at the bottom. Pick what's wrong, add a note if you like, and send. It's the fastest way to get a listing fixed.

Contact

For anything else, email evsndrs@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

A place's hours are wrong, or it closed.
Use Report wrong info on that place. We check hours against each business's own website and listings, but they change, and reports help most.

My favorite spot isn't in the app.
Email us its name and we'll take a look. We add places in Winston-Salem and nearby.

Where do events come from?
Public calendars from local organizations (such as Old Salem, NCMA Winston-Salem, the City of Winston-Salem and Wake Forest University), refreshed every night. Each event links back to its source.

Trivia, music or a special listed under "Happening here" is out of date.
Weekly happenings change often. Each list shows when it was last checked; please report anything that's moved or ended.

"Near me" isn't working.
Go to iPhone Settings → Explore WS → Location and choose "While Using the App."

How do I delete my data?
Favorites live only on your phone, so deleting the app removes them. To delete a report you sent, email us. See the privacy policy for details.

For businesses and food trucks

Own a restaurant, bar, brewery, coffee shop or food truck in Winston-Salem? Email us to update your hours or details, or to list your weekly trivia, live music, specials or truck stops.
